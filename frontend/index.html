Synthetic OCT B-scan generation, annotation and figure export

OCTForge is a figure-authoring tool for teaching and scientific-style illustration. Everything you create is generated locally on your computer. There are no accounts, and your scenes, settings and exports stay on your machine. Your browser contacts Google Fonts to load the Outfit typeface.

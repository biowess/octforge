About OCTForge

A figure-authoring tool that generates synthetic retinal OCT B-scans for teaching, lecture decks, course materials and scientific-style illustration.

Version 1.0.0 · Local-first · No account required

Every image is synthetic Every image OCTForge produces is synthetic. It is procedurally generated from a parametric forward model. It is not derived from, and does not represent, any real patient. OCTForge is not a diagnostic device and has not been validated for clinical decision-making.

What OCTForge is

Optical coherence tomography (OCT) is a standard imaging method in ophthalmology, but teaching cases are hard to build from real scans: they carry patient identifiers, consent constraints and inconsistent quality. OCTForge lets an educator describe a retina, add a pathology, choose an acquisition setup and produce a clean, reproducible cross-section that matches the question being taught.

A scene is fully described by its settings, and a fixed seed reproduces the same image every time. Scenes can be annotated, measured, composed into multi-panel figures, saved as projects and exported in formats suited to slides, print and the web.

How the renderer works

The renderer is a procedurally and physically motivated approximation. It follows the general structure of how light interacts with retinal tissue. It is not a clinical-grade physical simulation, and its outputs should be read as illustrations, not measurements of a real eye.

  1. Parametric anatomy. Foveal shape, boundary irregularity and per-layer thickness and reflectivity define the retina.
  2. Pathology deformation. Lesions such as cysts, fluid or drusen deform and re-reflect the anatomy.
  3. Optical forward model. Beer–Lambert attenuation and coherent, PSF-correlated speckle shape the signal.
  4. Acquisition and artifacts. Scan geometry, resolution, averaging and optional artifacts such as shadowing or motion are applied.
  5. Display mapping. Dynamic range, gamma and window level convert signal to the image you see.

Technology

BackendPython with FastAPI and Uvicorn, data models in Pydantic v2, numerical work in NumPy and SciPy, and image encoding in Pillow.
FrontendPlain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no framework and no build step. The interface is served by the same backend process.
DeploymentLocal-first. OCTForge runs on 127.0.0.1 and opens in your browser. No account, sign-in or internet connection is needed to render.
ProjectsSaved as JSON files in a projects/ folder on the machine running OCTForge.

Intended use and limits

OCTForge is designed for medical education, teaching cases, lecture slides, course materials and scientific-style figures. It is not designed for diagnosis, triage, treatment planning or any decision about a real patient. Do not present its output as a real clinical scan.

Built for educators OCTForge exists so that teachers can show what retinal disease looks like on OCT without depending on archives of real patient scans. If you use it in teaching, label synthetic images as synthetic.
